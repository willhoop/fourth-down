# Tutorial: get your first fourth-down call

This tutorial takes 5 minutes. You need a web browser. You do not need Python.

## 1. Open the app

1. Double-click **Fourth Down Engine** on the desktop.
2. If the shortcut is not there, open `app/index.html` in the project folder.

The app opens on the **Decision** tab. It shows a sample situation: down 3,
4:30 left in the fourth quarter, 4th & 3 at the opponent's 34.

Click **Get the call**.

## 2. Read the call

1. Look at **The call**. It shows the best option in large letters.
2. Read the line below it. It gives the edge in win-probability points.
3. Read the **Confidence** line. It tells you how many of the 20 resampled
   models agree.
4. Look at the three bars. Each bar is your chance to win after that choice.

## 3. Change the situation

1. Click **Edit situation**.
2. Set **Yards to go** to 8. Click **Get the call**. The call can change to a
   field goal.
3. Click **Edit situation**. Set **Clock** to 0 min, 4 sec. Click **Get the
   call**. With 4 seconds left and down 3, only the field goal ties.
4. Click **Edit situation**. Click **2nd** down. Click **Get the call**. With 4
   seconds left, the app tells you to kick now, not run a play.

## 4. Turn on a factor

1. Click **Edit situation**. Turn on **Weather**.
2. Move **Wind** to 25 mph. Click **Get the call**.
3. Look at the field-goal bar. Wind makes a long kick less likely.

## 5. Read the map

1. Look at the **Decision map**. Each square is one spot and one distance, with
   your score, clock and factors.
2. Green is go, gold is field goal, blue is punt. A faded square is a toss-up.
3. Tap a square. The app loads that situation and gives its call.
