# How to use the app

## Set the situation

1. Enter **Our score** and **Their score**.
2. Click the **Quarter**.
3. Enter the **Clock** (minutes and seconds left in the quarter). The app shows
   if the two-minute warning is still to come.
4. Click **Own** or **Opp** and set the yard line (1–50).
5. Click the **Down**. The app starts on 4th down.
6. Set **Yards to go**.
7. Click the number of timeouts for each team.
8. Click **Home** or **Away**.
9. In the first half, click who gets the second-half kickoff.
10. Click **Get the call**. The app shows the results screen. To change the
    situation, click **Edit situation**. Your inputs stay.

Use the **−** and **+** buttons to change a number by 1. Hold a button to
repeat. The seconds and the yard line also have **−5** and **+5** buttons. The
app calculates only when you click **Get the call**.

On 1st, 2nd or 3rd down the app compares **Run a play** with **Kick a FG now**.
Use this when the clock is about to run out.

## Set the factors

Each factor has a switch. A factor that is off uses a neutral value.

| Factor | When on, set | When off, the engine uses |
|---|---|---|
| Weather | Wind (mph), temperature (°F), rain or snow | No wind, 70 °F, dry |
| Stadium | Roof (outdoors or dome), altitude | An average stadium |
| Team strength | Pre-game point spread, as a betting line (−7 = you are 7-point favorites) | An even matchup |
| Kicker | A kicker, league average, or replacement level | A league-average kicker |

A dome cancels weather. The kicker list shows kickers with at least 10
attempts in the last two seasons.

## Read the output

Each bar is the chance to win **if you choose that option**. The bars are
alternatives. They do not add up to 100%.

- **The call**: the option with the highest win probability. "Toss-up" means
  the edge is under 1 point.
- **Confidence**: the number of the 20 resampled models that agree. 18 or more
  is a confident call.
- **Detail boxes**: the odds and win probability behind each option.
- **Decision map**: the call at every spot and distance for the current score,
  clock and factors.

## See the grades

1. Click **Coaches**. Click a column name to sort. Click a coach to see his
   seasons and costliest calls.
2. Click **Kickers**. The table shows current kickers and the replacement level.

For what the grades mean, read the white paper, section 5.
